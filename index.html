A multi-criteria audio deepfake evaluation, built for scrutiny — not just a score.

Upload a clip and VERDICT runs it through classification (AASIST), explanation (SHAP-based attribution), and three independently-reported scores: Robustness, Explainability, and Legal-Alignment. Nothing here is folded into one blended number — see exactly where a result is strong and where it isn't.
